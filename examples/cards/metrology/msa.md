# Measurement system analysis

## Q: What does a Gage R&R study measure, and when is the result acceptable? ^me-001
DE: Was bewertet eine Messsystemanalyse (Gage R&R), und wann ist das Ergebnis akzeptabel?
- **Repeatability** (equipment variation, EV): same operator, same part, same gauge, repeated.
- **Reproducibility** (appraiser variation, AV): different operators measuring the same parts.

Acceptance criteria per AIAG MSA for %GRR: **< 10 %** acceptable, **10–30 %** conditionally
acceptable, **> 30 %** not acceptable. The number of distinct categories should be **ndc ≥ 5**.

## Q: What does the "rule of ten" in metrology say? ^me-002
DE: Was besagt die „Goldene Regel der Messtechnik“ (10:1-Regel)?
The resolution of a measuring instrument (and ideally its uncertainty) should be at most
**1/10 of the tolerance** of the feature being measured.
