# Manufacturing processes (DIN 8580)

## Q: What are the six main groups of manufacturing processes according to DIN 8580? ^mf-001
1. **Urformen**: primary shaping (casting, injection moulding, additive manufacturing)
2. **Umformen**: forming (bending, deep drawing, forging)
3. **Trennen**: separating/cutting (turning, milling, drilling, laser cutting)
4. **Fügen**: joining (screwing, welding, bonding, press-fitting)
5. **Beschichten**: coating (painting, anodising, electroplating)
6. **Stoffeigenschaft ändern**: changing material properties (hardening, annealing)

## Term: Urformen = primary shaping ^mf-002
Creating a solid body from shapeless material, e.g. casting, injection moulding or 3D printing.

## Cloze: Classify the process ^mf-003
Injection moulding belongs to {{primary shaping (Urformen)}}, bending belongs to {{forming (Umformen)}} and milling belongs to {{separating (Trennen)}}.
