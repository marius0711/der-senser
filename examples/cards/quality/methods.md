# Quality methods

## Q: What is the RPN in an FMEA, and why did the AIAG-VDA handbook replace it? ^qa-001
DE: Was ist die RPZ (Risikoprioritätszahl) in einer FMEA, und warum hat das AIAG-VDA-Handbuch sie ersetzt?
**RPN = S × O × D** (severity × occurrence × detection, each rated 1–10, so 1–1000).

The AIAG-VDA FMEA handbook (2019) replaced it with the **Action Priority (AP)** rating
(high/medium/low). The RPN weights the three factors equally, so a high severity can be
hidden behind a low overall number.

## Sequence: The 8D problem-solving steps ^qa-002
DE: Die Schritte der 8D-Methode
1. D1: Form the team
2. D2: Describe the problem
3. D3: Implement containment actions
4. D4: Find the root cause
5. D5: Choose and verify corrective actions
6. D6: Implement and validate corrective actions
7. D7: Prevent recurrence
8. D8: Recognise the team
