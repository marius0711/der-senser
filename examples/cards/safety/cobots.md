# Collaborative robot safety

## Q: What are the four collaborative operation modes? ^sa-001
DE: Welche vier Arten der Mensch-Roboter-Kollaboration gibt es?
1. **Safety-rated monitored stop**
2. **Hand guiding**
3. **Speed and separation monitoring** (SSM)
4. **Power and force limiting** (PFL)

(ISO 10218, detailed in ISO/TS 15066)

## Cloze: Power and force limiting ^sa-002
DE: Biomechanische Grenzwerte für den Kontakt mit dem menschlichen Körper wurden in der {{ISO/TS 15066}} veröffentlicht. Sie unterscheidet {{transienten}} Kontakt (kurzer Stoß, die Person kann ausweichen) und {{quasistatischen}} Kontakt (Klemmen).
Biomechanical limit values for contact with the human body were published in {{ISO/TS 15066}}.
It distinguishes {{transient}} contact (short impact, the person can move away) from
{{quasi-static}} contact (clamping).

## Q: Which Performance Level do safety functions of industrial robots typically need? ^sa-003
DE: Welchen Performance Level brauchen Sicherheitsfunktionen von Industrierobotern typischerweise?
Usually **PL d with Category 3** according to ISO 13849-1, unless a risk assessment shows otherwise.
PL ranges from a (lowest) to e (highest).
