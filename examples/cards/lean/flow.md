# Flow and productivity

## Cloze: OEE ^le-001
OEE = {{availability}} × {{performance}} × {{quality}}.
A value of about {{85 %}} is often cited as world class.

## Q: What is takt time, and how does it differ from cycle time? ^le-002
**Takt time** = available production time / customer demand. It is the pace the customer requires.
Example: 450 min / 900 units = **30 s** per unit.

**Cycle time** is the time a process actually needs per unit. If cycle time is longer than
takt time, the line cannot meet demand.

## Term: Rüstzeit = changeover time ^le-003
Reduced with SMED (Single-Minute Exchange of Die) by converting internal setup steps into external ones.
