# Metals and magnets

## Q: Compare density and Young's modulus of steel and aluminium. What follows for lightweight design? ^ma-001
DE: Vergleiche Dichte und E-Modul von Stahl und Aluminium. Was folgt daraus für den Leichtbau?
| | Density | Young's modulus E |
|---|---|---|
| Steel | ≈ 7.85 g/cm³ | ≈ 210 GPa |
| Aluminium | ≈ 2.70 g/cm³ | ≈ 70 GPa |

The **specific stiffness** E/ρ is almost the same. Aluminium saves weight mainly because
thicker walls and larger cross-sections become possible, which raises bending stiffness.

## Cloze: NdFeB permanent magnets ^ma-002
DE: NdFeB-Magnete haben das höchste {{Energieprodukt (BH)max}} aller handelsüblichen Dauermagnete. Standard-N-Sorten sind auf etwa {{80 °C}} begrenzt, das Material neigt zu {{Korrosion}} und wird daher meist mit {{Nickel}} beschichtet.
NdFeB magnets have the highest {{energy product (BH)max}} of all commercial permanent magnets.
Standard N grades are limited to about {{80 °C}}, and the material is prone to {{corrosion}},
so it is usually {{nickel}}-coated.

## Term: Streckgrenze = yield strength ^ma-003
Stress at which plastic (permanent) deformation begins. Symbol R_e, or R_p0.2 for materials without a distinct yield point.
